AI Fitness Coach App - React Native

Project Vision

We are building an AI-powered fitness and health coach, not just another fitness tracker.

The app should become a personal fitness assistant that understands the user’s health, workouts, nutrition, sleep, and long-term goals through AI.

Instead of manually navigating through multiple screens, users should be able to simply ask questions in natural language.

Example:

“Why am I feeling tired today?”

The AI should analyze:

	•	Sleep
	•	Heart rate
	•	HRV
	•	Yesterday’s workout
	•	Calories
	•	Recovery
	•	Weight trend

and provide a personalized answer.

Overall Goal

The app should feel like having a personal AI fitness coach that combines the conversational experience of ChatGPT with the data awareness of Apple Health/Health Connect and the training intelligence of a professional coach. Every answer should be grounded in the user’s own health history and capable of taking actions—such as logging meals, generating workouts, updating goals, and tracking progress—through natural conversation.
