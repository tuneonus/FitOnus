=====================================
1. PURPOSE
=====================================

This document provides a high-level overview of the FitOnus product roadmap. It outlines the phased approach to development, from the initial MVP to future enterprise and social features.

=====================================
2. PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (MVP)
=====================================
Target: Launch

  - Core Architecture & Design System implementation.
  - Authentication & Onboarding flows.
  - AI Coach (basic chat, context awareness, text-based recommendations).
  - Basic Workout System (manual logging, predefined plans).
  - Basic Nutrition System (manual logging, simple food database).
  - Health Connect Integration (read steps, sleep).
  - Dashboard & User Profile.

=====================================
3. PHASE 2: INTELLIGENCE & AUTOMATION
=====================================
Target: Launch + 3 Months

  - AI Memory System (long-term fact extraction).
  - RAG System (evidence-based knowledge base).
  - Advanced Workout System (AI generation, PR detection, progressive overload).
  - Advanced Nutrition System (AI meal logging from text, barcode scanning).
  - Recovery System & Analytics.
  - Weekly/Monthly Reports.

=====================================
4. PHASE 3: ENGAGEMENT & MONETIZATION
=====================================
Target: Launch + 6 Months

  - Subscriptions (RevenueCat integration for Premium features).
  - Gamification (Streaks, Badges, Achievements).
  - Wearable Integrations (Apple Watch companion app, Garmin sync).
  - Progress Photo timelines and comparisons.
  - Advanced Notifications (smart scheduling).

=====================================
5. PHASE 4: SOCIAL & ECOSYSTEM
=====================================
Target: Launch + 12 Months

  - Social Feed (share workouts, PRs, and meals with friends).
  - Community Challenges (e.g., "100k steps in a week").
  - Recipe database and AI recipe generation.
  - Trainer Portal (allow human coaches to view client data and collaborate with the AI).
  - Video form analysis (AI computer vision).
